## Platform

## Users

Primary users are healthcare executives and senior operators who own data,
analytics, AI, product, operations, or transformation outcomes. The best-fit
buyers are health plans, provider organizations, care-navigation firms, wellness
companies, digital health teams, and healthcare-adjacent founders who need a
senior translator between business goals, regulated healthcare workflows, and
working data systems.

This is inferred from user-provided background: 21 years across payer,
provider, wellness, and navigation; deep data, analytics, and insights work;
dbt fluency; and recent practical AI system-building experience.

## Product Purpose

ref(health) Consulting sells senior healthcare data, analytics, and practical
AI help. Success is paid discovery, assessment, implementation, advisory, or
fractional leadership work that turns messy healthcare data and AI pressure into
usable, governed systems.

The flagship offer is helping a company build its Data AI Operating System:
the operating layer that connects business decisions, governed data products,
model/provider strategy, agentic workflows, measurement, documentation, and
handoff.

The primary conversion goal is revenue. The site should make a qualified buyer
want to email for a paid engagement, diagnostic, or proposal discussion.

## Positioning

ref(health) is not a generic AI implementation shop. It combines 21 years of
healthcare operating context with hands-on analytics engineering, dbt fluency,
business translation, and recent agentic AI product-building experience.

The distinctive mechanism is practical translation: start with the healthcare
decision, map the data and risk, build the smallest auditable system that can
change the work, and leave the client with documentation and ownership.

## Operating Context

The site must speak to buyers dealing with:

- Fragmented claims, clinical, member, provider, operational, and product data.
- AI pilots that need governance, ROI logic, data readiness, and workflow fit.
- Analytics teams struggling with trust, lineage, metric definitions, and
  business adoption.
- Payer and provider modernization pressure, including interoperability,
  prior authorization, provider data, quality, and care-navigation workflows.
- Leaders who need an experienced data executive, not a large consulting bench.

Market research used for positioning points to demand for AI-ready data
foundations, data governance, responsible AI, payer modernization, provider data
modernization, workflow automation, and decision intelligence.

## Product Principles

- Sell the business outcome before the technical stack.
- Use healthcare-specific stakes, data types, and workflows instead of generic
  AI language.
- Make AI credible by pairing it with governance, data readiness, auditability,
  and workflow adoption.
- Position the Data AI Operating System as the mechanism that makes AI useful,
  governable, and measurable inside a real company.
- Package services as paid offers buyers can understand quickly.
- Keep claims defensible, sourced, and confidentiality-safe.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The site should use semantic HTML, visible keyboard focus, responsive layouts,
high contrast, readable line lengths, and clear contact paths. The primary CTA
must not depend on color alone.
