Turn AI enthusiasm into a funding decision.

A focused diagnostic creates a shared view across the founder, product, data, engineering, clinical, and operating teams. It answers what can move now, what needs groundwork, and what should not be built yet.

  • Use-case valueWhat decision, revenue, cost, quality, risk, or adoption problem would improve?

The focused diagnostic is usually a one-to-two-week engagement and starts at $2,500. The exact scope depends on the number of use cases, systems, stakeholders, and decisions in play.

How much does the diagnostic cost?

The focused health-tech startup diagnostic starts at $2,500. If the work requires a broader data landscape, more stakeholders, or a larger implementation plan, the scope and price are agreed before work begins.

Do we need to choose an AI provider first?

No. The assessment can recommend a multi-provider model strategy or a deep single-provider path based on the work, policy, integration, delivery speed, and current commitments.

What happens after the assessment?

You get a clear next decision. That may be a build sprint, a healthcare data landscape assessment, a dbt accelerator, a Data AI Operating System build, or a decision to wait until the groundwork is ready.
