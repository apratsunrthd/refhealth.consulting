Why use dbt in a healthcare data environment?

dbt provides a shared workflow for versioned SQL models, tests, documentation, lineage, review, and ownership. That makes critical metrics easier to inspect and easier for business teams to trust.

Can you work with our existing warehouse?

Yes. The starting point is the current data environment and the decisions it needs to support. The engagement can improve the dbt project and workflow without assuming a platform migration.

Is this only for a large analytics team?

No. A small team can benefit from clear conventions, tests, documentation, and handoff. The scope should match the critical data products and the people who maintain them.
